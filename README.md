## 📂 Featured Projects

### 📡 [Telecom Customer Churn Analysis](https://github.com/pardisHpa/telecom-churn-rfm-kmeans)
> MSc Research Dissertation — Real-world telecom data analysis at scale

- Analysed **1,047,058 transactions** from Iran's leading mobile operator (Hamrah-e-Aval)
- Segmented **213,372 customers** into 5 behavioral groups using RFM + K-Means Clustering
- Discovered that **23.7% of customers drive 73.1% of total revenue**
- Benchmarked RFM-K-Means against Random Forest, SVM, Logistic Regression & Decision Tree
- `Python` `scikit-learn` `K-Means Clustering` `RFM Analysis` `pandas` `seaborn`

### 🛒 [Superstore Sales EDA](https://github.com/pardisHpa/Superstore-Sales-EDA)
> End-to-end Exploratory Data Analysis on US retail transactions (2015–2018)

- Analysed **9,800 transactions** across 4 US regions and 3 product categories
- Identified **South region underperformance** — consistently 40% below West
- Uncovered Q4 seasonality — November averages **6x higher** revenue than February
- Found Home Office as highest avg order value segment despite being the smallest group
- `Python` `pandas` `matplotlib` `seaborn` `EDA` `Business Insights`
